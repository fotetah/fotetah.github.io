Grant Seeker — Use and scope notice

Grant Seeker is a private, single-owner grant-research and correspondence-tracking tool. The public information site does not offer account creation, grant applications, payment collection or a public hosted software service.

The tool identifies research candidates and messages requiring review. It does not guarantee grant eligibility, funding, application acceptance or payment. The owner must verify source terms, deadlines, sender identity and financial outcomes. Every application submission, attestation, signature or fee requires a separate owner decision; this notice does not authorize any such action.

Use of Google, Hostinger, Airtable, GitHub and ChatGPT remains subject to the owner's agreements with those services. This notice does not accept funder terms or create a public service agreement with website visitors.

Questions about the private tool: fotetech@gmail.com. See the Grant Seeker privacy notice for Google-data handling, service providers, retention and revocation.
